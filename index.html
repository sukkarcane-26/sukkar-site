Our Story

The Beginning

I grew up in Egypt, and sugarcane juice was just part of life there. You'd see a vendor on almost every corner with a press, and they'd hand you a cup, or just a plastic bag with a straw in it, and that was your drink. It wasn't a treat, it was normal. I didn't think twice about it until I moved to the US and realized I couldn't find it anywhere. Not at a grocery store, not at a juice bar, nowhere. I looked for a long time.

Eventually I stopped looking and decided to just make it myself. I found a press, figured out how to source good cane, and started setting up a pop-up, not knowing if anyone outside of Egypt would even want to try it. People lined up. A few of them told me "it tastes like back home, and even better," and that's when I knew this was worth doing for real.

Our Mission

Everything I sell is pressed fresh, right there at the pop-up, in front of you. No concentrate, no shortcuts, nothing sitting in a bottle for weeks. Since this is still a very new business, I press every stalk myself, and honestly I like it that way. It keeps me close to the product and close to the people buying it.

My goal has never been complicated. I want to make the best sugarcane juice you can get outside of Egypt, and I want it to give someone a taste of home, whether that home is Egypt, Pakistan, or somewhere else entirely. This is still the beginning for us, just me and a pop-up, and I'm building it one event at a time. Wherever it grows from here, I want it to keep feeling like the same thing it started as: something simple, something real, and something that makes your day a little sweeter.
